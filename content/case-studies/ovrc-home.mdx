## Challenge

Integrators wanted to give homeowners similar control to what they had with OvrC. Homeowners had paid tens of thousands for custom setups. DIY competitors — Disney Circle, Nest, Sonos — were entering the market and threatening the custom integration industry.

OvrC was never designed for homeowner consumption. We had to build something **simple for dealers to set up** and even simpler for homeowners to use.

## Discovery

Integrators wanted to stop getting late-night calls for simple reboots. Homeowners needed network visibility. Integrators wanted **white-label branding** — their logo, their contact info, their brand.

![OvrC Home end user app wireframes with detailed annotations](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-end-user-wireframes.png)

## Vision

We created **macros** — a series of commands to multiple devices triggered by a single button. Instead of knowing which device to reboot, homeowners just tap "Reset Internet" and the system handles the rest.

Built dealer branding: logo upload, contact info, customizable support screens. Named the app **"OvrC Home."**

![OvrC Home command grid letting homeowners fix problems with a single tap](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-commands.png)

## Dealer Setup

The dealer experience was critical. We built the setup flow directly into the OvrC web platform, allowing integrators to configure commands, add users, and customize branding for each customer location.

![OvrC customer access setup wireframes showing the dealer configuration workflow](/images/ovrc/ovrc-customer-access-wireframes.png)

## Launch

Paired with OvrC Pro device launch (service model initiative). Initial pairing didn't go as planned — integrators struggled to pitch the OvrC Pro device to end consumers. We severed the requirement and saw greater attachment.

![OvrC Home app troubleshooting screen](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-app-troubleshooting.png)

## Parental Controls

Added **"Parental Controls"** feature with profile-based device management, schedules, and content filtering. This became a key differentiator against consumer competitors.

![OvrC Home parental controls wireframes](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-parental-controls-wireframes.png)

![OvrC Home dashboard with wireless profiles and security settings](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-dashboard.png)

### Profiles & Schedules

Each family member gets a profile with associated devices. Parents can pause wireless access and set schedules for homework time, bedtime, or dinner.

![OvrC Home profile detail showing pause wireless and schedule controls](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-profile.png)

![OvrC Home schedule settings for setting daily pause/resume times](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-schedule.png)

### Security & Content Filtering

Three tiers of content filtering — from basic SafeSearch to medium filtering of adult and explicit content.

![OvrC Home security settings with content filtering tiers](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-security.png)

## Branded Support

Each dealer gets their own branded support screen with company logo, phone number, and email — making the app feel like it belongs to the integrator, not SnapAV.

![OvrC Home branded support contact screen for Tech Integrators](/images/ovrc-home/ovrc-home-support.png)

## Impact

- **Tens of thousands** of locations using OvrC Home
- **100,000+** macros created by integrators
- Healthy portion of users charging end users for the service
- Converting truck rolls into **better-margin jobs**
- Positioned custom integrators to compete with DIY smart home solutions
