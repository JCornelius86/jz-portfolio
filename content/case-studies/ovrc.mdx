## Challenge

Contacted by a small audio/video hardware company (SnapAV) to build a cloud app for their IP-connected power conditioners so integrators could reset them remotely. Remote management had been the "holy grail" of the CEDIA industry. Accepted the position as **UX Lead** on a team of five.

![WattBox power conditioner - the hardware that started it all](/images/ovrc/ovrc-wattbox-product.jpg)

## Approach

Built a focused framework for managing multiple customers, each with multiple devices. Settled on four critical pillars: **monitor, control, configure, and notify**. Started with three power conditioner models. Grew to 200+ native models.

![OvrC platform sitemap showing the full information architecture](/images/ovrc/ovrc-sitemap.png)

## Discovery

Leveraged SnapAV's great customer relationships. Traveled to conferences, tested prototypes, iterated. Discovered dealers needed outlet on/off, reboot, and configuration. Created "Network Device" outlet categorization to prevent accidental shutoffs.

![OvrC at a CEDIA trade show booth](/images/ovrc-trade-show.jpg)

## Vision

Decided to make the platform easy enough that anyone could learn, even sacrificing some functionality. Mantra: **"Make our customers successful by building what they need. No more, no less."**

Our goals:
- Keep integrators informed
- Make troubleshooting simple from any device
- Listen to customers and make them successful

![OvrC customer dashboard with network health overview](/images/ovrc/ovrc-dashboard.png)

## Launch

Launched in 2014 with immediate CEDIA adoption. Challenges arose with feature requests as internal stakeholders wanted additional device types — surveillance, networking, media.

![OvrC mobile app showing the device list for a customer location](/images/ovrc/ovrc-app-device-list.png)

## Research

Championed usability testing culture at SnapAV. Created a style guide for consistent local UIs across all product lines. Moved away from JDM (Joint Development Manufacturing) model.

![OvrC mobile wireframes showing early design exploration](/images/ovrc/ovrc-mobile-wireframes.jpg)

## Growth

As OvrC expanded beyond power management, we added networking, surveillance, and media categories. The platform needed to scale without losing its simplicity.

![OvrC map view showing customer distribution across the US](/images/ovrc/ovrc-customer-map.png)

![Detailed customer map view centered on the Charlotte, NC area](/images/ovrc/ovrc-map-view.png)

## Feature Expansion

We developed advanced features like WAN settings, billing integration, and video distribution — all while maintaining the core principle of simplicity.

![OvrC advanced WAN settings configuration](/images/ovrc/ovrc-wan-settings.png)

![OvrC billing feature wireframes](/images/ovrc/ovrc-billing-wireframes.png)

![OvrC MOIP video distribution wireframes](/images/ovrc/ovrc-moip-wireframes.png)

## Impact

OvrC became the **premier remote management system** in CEDIA.

- **5+ million** devices at **200,000+** locations worldwide
- Grew from **3 models to 200+** across power, networking, surveillance, and media
- Introduced a hub for non-SnapAV device visibility
- Built and led the UX team from **1 to a full design organization**
